# Portfolio Video — HeyGen Script & Prompt

**Goal:** Premium clients laana jo apna Instagram handle karwana chahte hain / edited videos (gym, influencer, AI videos) chahte hain.
**Length:** ~45–50 sec | **Format:** 9:16 vertical (Reels) | **Language:** Hinglish — normal, professional, casual
**Look:** Dark background, Red + White glow (Option A) ya Sky Blue glow (Option B)

---

## 1. HeyGen Avatar / Scene Prompt (copy-paste)

```
Vertical 9:16 talking-head video. Confident, well-dressed creator (black t-shirt or blazer),
sitting in a dark modern studio with soft rim light. Background: deep black with subtle
red neon glow (or sky-blue glow) behind the shoulders, slight bokeh. Camera: medium close-up,
eye level, slow subtle push-in. Tone: calm, premium, confident — not salesy, not over-excited.
Natural hand gestures, slight smile, direct eye contact. Speak in Hinglish, medium pace,
clear pauses between lines. Studio-quality audio, no background music from avatar.
```

**Voice settings:** Male/female (aapki choice), calm-confident, speed 1.0–1.05x, Hinglish/Hindi-English voice.
